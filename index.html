Click a pocket to select it, then click a search result to put a card there. Click a card to look at it up close. Ctrl+click a card to remove it, hover and press Ctrl+D to copy it. C closes the binder; click a binder on the table to swap to it, or an empty slot to start a new one. Drag a page by its outer edge to turn it, or type a page number to turn there. Right-drag to tilt the view, middle-drag to pan, scroll to zoom, R to reset the view, T to see the whole table.
Move the mouse to tilt. Drag sideways to spin.
